What is WordGallows?

WordGallows is a word-guessing game with a twist: it can generate fresh themed words on the fly, each with a hint and a fun fact. Pick a theme and difficulty, guess letter by letter, build streaks, and level up. It also works offline with a built-in word library.

Frequently asked questions

New words are not loading

  • Generating new themed words needs an internet connection. Check that you are online.
  • There is a daily limit on freshly generated words. When you reach it, the game keeps going using its built-in offline word library.
  • If you are offline, WordGallows still plays using the offline library, so you can keep guessing.

Is any of my information sent when words are generated?

No personal information is sent. Only the theme, difficulty, and language are sent to generate a word. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How do streaks, XP, and coins work?

Winning rounds builds your streak and earns XP and coins, which you can spend on cosmetics. All of this progress is saved on your device.

How do I change the language, difficulty, or theme?

Open Settings to change the app language, default difficulty, and visual theme. You choose the word theme each time you start a round.

My data and resetting the app

All of your stats and progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes this data permanently.
