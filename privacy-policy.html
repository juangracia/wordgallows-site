Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 18, 2026

WordGallows is developed by Juan Gracia. The short version: WordGallows has no accounts and no analytics, and it never collects your personal information. To generate fresh themed words it sends only the theme, difficulty, and language to a word-generation service, never anything about you.

Information stored on your device

WordGallows saves your game data locally on your device so it remembers your progress:

  • Game statistics (games played, win rate, streaks).
  • Your progress (XP, level, coins) and any equipped cosmetics.
  • Daily reward and daily challenge state.
  • Your preferences (language, difficulty, theme).

This information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes this data.

Word generation (network access)

WordGallows can generate new themed words using an AI word-generation service. When it does, it sends only:

  • The theme for the round (for example "space" or a theme you pick).
  • The difficulty and language setting.
  • A random, app-generated device identifier used only to apply a daily limit on requests. It is not linked to your identity, your Apple or Google account, or any advertising profile.

These requests are processed by Anthropic (the Claude AI service) to return a word, a hint, and a fun fact. No personal information is ever sent. If you are offline or reach the daily limit, the app falls back to a built-in offline word library, so it keeps working without a connection.

Information we do not collect

WordGallows does not:

  • Collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.
  • Use accounts or require sign-in.
  • Include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party advertising software development kits.

Children's privacy

WordGallows is family-friendly and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Generated words are constrained to be appropriate for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
